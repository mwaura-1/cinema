PULP FICTION

        CLICK THE IMAGE FOR THE MOVIE TRAILER.

        Pulp Fiction is a 1994 American crime film written and directed by Quentin Tarantino, who conceived it with
        Roger Avary. Starring John Travolta, Samuel L. Jackson, Bruce Willis, Tim Roth, Ving Rhames, and Uma Thurman,
        it tells several stories of criminal Los Angeles. The film's title refers to the pulp magazines and hardboiled
        crime novels popular during the mid-20th century, known for their graphic violence and punchy dialogue.

            Almost Famous is a 2000 American coming-of-age comedy-drama film written and directed by Cameron Crowe, 
            inspired by his own teenage years as a music journalist. The film follows a 15-year-old aspiring writer
            who lands a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to tour with a rising rock band in the 1970s. As he navigates fame,
            music, and blurred lines between honesty and loyalty, the film captures the magic, chaos, and soul of rock culture.
            Known for its warmth, nostalgia, and iconic soundtrack, Almost Famous is a love letter to music, youth, and finding your voice.

BLACK SWAN

    CLICK THE IMAGE FOR THE MOVIE TRAILER.
    
        Black Swan is a 2010 American psychological thriller film directed by Darren Aronofsky and starring Natalie Portman. 
        The film follows a dedicated ballerina whose pursuit of perfection leads her into a dark and unsettling mental 
        unraveling as she prepares for a demanding dual role in Swan Lake. Blending psychological horror with intense 
        physical performance, the film explores themes of obsession, identity, and self-destruction. Renowned for its 
        haunting visuals and Portman’s Academy Award-winning performance, Black Swan is a chilling portrait of ambition 
        taken to its extreme.
